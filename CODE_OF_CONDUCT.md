# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate professionally, discuss technical disagreement in
good faith, respect security and privacy boundaries, and avoid sharing another
person's private data, credentials, sessions, or local machine details.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and knowingly unsafe disclosure
of sensitive material are not acceptable. Report concerns to the project owner
through an approved private channel with only the information needed to
investigate. The project owner may clarify expectations, remove content, limit
participation, or take other proportionate action.
